Early-access privacy notice

This is a development preview and must not be deployed publicly or used to collect live prospects without a reviewed legal notice, real contact channel, approved retention/deletion mechanism, and secure production infrastructure.

Data entered

Hotel name and area, optional address and room count, contact person's name, role and business email, selected workflow challenge, early-access opt-in timestamp, separate optional promotional opt-in and withdrawal proof. The development database stores submitted contact information locally, not in GitHub.

Purpose

Review requests for product updates and design-partner conversations. Promotional communications require a separate optional choice. An opt-in does not mean a hotel is qualified or accepted for a pilot.

Withdrawal

The original browser response includes a private withdrawal token. Keep it safe and use the withdrawal form. The prototype records withdrawn status and disables marketing preferences; production must add proper data deletion, email verification, data-access procedures and contact information for the controller.

Storage and processing

This development service uses a local SQLite file. Do not submit real personal information to this prototype or expose it publicly. Before launch, require secure hosted storage, bounded retention, staff access policies, monitored anti-abuse, appropriate transparency and a reviewed Nigeria Data Protection Act compliance programme.
