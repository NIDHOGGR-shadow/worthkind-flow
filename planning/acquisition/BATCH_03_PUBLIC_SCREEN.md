## Buyer and booking hypotheses — unverified

- **Better fit to investigate:** Proof Hotel & Lounge (`L099`, publicly described as 19 rooms), Terraform, The Metropolis, Angle Villa, KingsCelia, Hotel Medallion, StoneHouse, Rooken and Haven. Ask about who owns a booking enquiry after a website/WhatsApp conversation.
- **Existing digital operations:** Ikad Yaba and Victoria Island have group booking information, RJ Hotels describes direct booking/instant email confirmation, and Homegate has an apparent booking search. Do not pitch an overhaul where their existing flow may work.
- **Multi-property buyers:** Ikad, RJ/NSPRI, Lustro and Heights may produce several physical-property records but one procurement decision. Distinguish **physical-property screening** from count of buying organizations.
- **Outside / borderline:** Limewood reports only 8 units; Lustro Lagos has a reported 10 suites but is still pending direct verification. Don't count either as initially size-qualified.
- **Source limitations:** Latest web crawls and third-party business listings cannot prove real hotel availability, live payment verification, owner contact authority or a recent internal complaint. All are questions for discovery, not assertions.
