## 1. Purpose

The parties wish to explore a potential limited hotel guest-enquiry and reception-workflow pilot for **Worthkind Flow**, a hospitality software product still under development. The goal of the discussion is to evaluate whether guest enquiries can be routed reliably to authorized reception staff without requiring guests to use AI.

The product is not yet connected to the hotel's live PMS, payment gateway, WhatsApp Business account, telephone switchboard, kitchen or dispatch system.

## 2. Discovery activities

If both parties consent, the hotel may:
- Assign a manager and one reservations/reception representative for a short workflow discussion.
- Explain its existing approved public website, enquiry channels and reservation process at a non-sensitive level.
- Identify a relevant pain point and current baseline (for example, enquiry acknowledgement time or percentage of assigned requests answered).
- Review a demo using **synthetic data**, without exposing real guest contact details.
- Discuss a possible small, supervised pilot and needed technical/legal approvals.

Worthkind may show the development prototype, document requirements, estimate a proposed integration scope and prepare a future pilot plan.

## 4. No implied commitments

This document is a **non-binding expression of interest** only. It is not an order, service-level agreement, data processing agreement, payment authorization, implementation contract, exclusivity commitment or guarantee of improved bookings. Neither side is obliged to launch or pay for a pilot by signing this expression. The parties will agree separately on pricing, support, intellectual property, confidentiality, data security, contract duration, incident handling and termination before a real pilot.

No live guest data, financial credentials, PMS credentials or customer communications may be accessed or processed under this expression alone.
