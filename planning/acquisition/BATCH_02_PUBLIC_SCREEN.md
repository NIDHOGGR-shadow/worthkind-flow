## Buyer and pitch implications

- **The George (61), Bon Voyage (85), Lilygate (74):** documented size fit and hotel-operated official sites. Next qualify operator and actual problem, not assumed lack of booking software.
- **GoldenSix, Thalia, Westine, Vintano:** live published enquiry/booking pages, so an add-on staff service workflow could be more relevant than a replacement website. Verify source-of-truth reservation integration only with approval.
- **Nordic and Radisson:** brand/chain operations may imply longer procurement. Ask who can authorize a pilot.
- **The View Lekki:** 12 confirmed rooms, outside current 15–100-room preference; don't erase, just deprioritize.

**Batch 02 independent evidence gate:** No new opt-ins, interviews or written commitments reported, because no authorized human contact occurred. Next batch should favor Yaba/smaller Lekki/Ikeja and hotel groups not already represented, verifying rooms and management access.
