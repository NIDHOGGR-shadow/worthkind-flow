# Worthkind Flow — Hotel Outreach and Design-Partner Sales Playbook

**Status:** ready for founder-led calls; **no calls have been placed by this workflow.**
**Goal:** 20 verified early-access opt-ins, 10 completed discovery interviews, 3 written non-binding pilot expressions of interest. Existing tracking: [GitHub issue #28](https://github.com/NIDHOGGR-shadow/worthkind/issues/28).

## The actual offer

Worthkind Flow is **in development**, initially focused on helping hotels route guest enquiries through a website and optional agent into a **human-owned reception queue**. It is NOT yet a production PMS, paid booking gateway, 24/7 call agent or fully working dispatch system.

**Design-partner invitation:** meet briefly, identify one measurable enquiry/guest-service issue, see the development demo, optionally propose a small trial with staff review and no unauthorized PMS integration. Hotel management decides whether to proceed, subject to a separate agreement.

Don't say: "We've automated your hotel", "we guarantee more bookings", "our AI replaces reception", "free forever", "we integrate with all PMS systems", or "you have a bad website". Do say: "We're evaluating whether this could help your operations, and your existing reservation system stays authoritative."

## 45-second discovery-first cold call

> Good morning. My name is [your name], working with Worthkind Flow. We're speaking with a small number of Lagos hotel managers to understand how guest enquiries move from calls, WhatsApp and a website to reception.
>
> I'm not calling to sell a ready-made booking system. Could you help me reach the person responsible for reservations or hotel operations? I'd like to ask them three questions and see whether a small early-access pilot would be useful.

If decision-maker answers:

> When a guest messages your hotel while reception is busy, how does the team make sure the enquiry is answered and followed through? Have you had a case recently where a message or booking request had to be chased manually?

Wait for their real example. Ask permission for a 15-minute discovery conversation instead of pitching the entire future system.

## Discovery interview (15–20 minutes)

1. What channels bring real reservations and questions today? Ask for approximate **weekly** volume and current recording method.
2. Can you walk me through a recent booking enquiry from first message to staff confirmation?
3. Which part requires the most manual work, and who is responsible? What was the last failure or delay?
4. Which property management system, booking engine, website, email/WhatsApp tools and payment provider do you already use? Who can approve integration?
5. How do staff mark an enquiry as accepted, answered or escalated? Is there an audit/history or shared queue?
6. If a guest wants a real human immediately, how can they bypass any automated flow?
7. Have you tried a chatbot, WhatsApp automation or receptionist software before? What worked or failed?
8. Would a demo of **guest enquiry → reception acceptance → guest reply** be relevant? Which metric would decide if a pilot is useful?
9. Who approves a pilot and what are their availability, privacy and procurement requirements?
10. May we send you one follow-up about an optional design-partner session? Confirm contact channel, frequency and permission.

Record quotes and facts in a **permissioned CRM**, not GitHub.

## Pilot invitation after validation

> Based on what you described, we could evaluate one narrow workflow: enquiries from your hotel website appear in a staff queue, reception takes ownership and replies, and the guest can choose a human without talking to AI. Would you be willing to review a demonstration and help define whether it's valuable? No live PMS or payment access is required for this initial discussion.

If interested, collect **explicit early-access permission** separately from marketing. A meeting is not a waitlist opt-in unless they affirm the communication permission, and interest is not a written pilot commitment.

## Permission-based WhatsApp follow-up

> Hello, thank you for speaking with me about how [Hotel] currently handles guest enquiries. As agreed, here's the Worthkind Flow early-access summary. We're building a guest enquiry and reception-handoff workflow and would value your feedback. Would [specific day/time] suit a 15-minute walkthrough? No hotel integration is connected yet. If you'd prefer no further product updates, reply STOP and I won't follow up.

Send **only after the recipient agrees** to a WhatsApp follow-up, and respect opt-outs.

## Personalized email

**Subject:** Guest enquiry workflow discussion for [Hotel]

Hello [Title/Name],

Thank you for taking a moment to discuss [specific operation observed]. I'm working on Worthkind Flow, a developing hospitality service tool intended to connect website enquiries with accountable hotel staff, while preserving direct human assistance.

We're inviting a few hotels to help evaluate a limited enquiry-to-reception pilot. The product has not yet been connected to a live reservation system or payment provider.

Would you be open to a 15-minute conversation about your current workflow and what a useful pilot would need to prove?

Regards,
Worthkind Flow Team

## Short follow-up rhythm (only if appropriate and permitted)

- Day 0: First permission-based conversation and agreed next step.
- Day 2–3: Follow-up once with the exact requested materials or a specific calendar option.
- Day 7: One respectful final check if follow-up permission remains; then pause.
- Immediately: stop marketing/update contact if declined or opted out.

## Common objections and grounded responses

| Buyer concern | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "We already have a website." | "That's fine. We're not necessarily replacing it. We're exploring whether enquiries and staff follow-ups are reliable across channels." |
| "We already have a chatbot or WhatsApp bot." | "Then your process may already solve it. Would you share what it handles well, and where human staff still step in? We won't assume a gap." |
| "Does your AI confirm paid bookings?" | "Not in this first pilot. Only your authorized booking/PMS and payment provider can confirm a reservation. We begin with enquiries and staff handoff." |
| "What does it cost?" | "The initial discovery conversation is free. Pilot scope and any fees must be agreed in writing after we understand your workflows. No undisclosed subscription." |
| "What about guest data?" | "We won't request production guest data for the first discovery meeting; a real deployment would require consent/privacy, access controls and a specific hotel agreement." |
| "No time." | "Could I ask just one question: what typically happens when a website or WhatsApp guest enquiry arrives after a staff shift?" |
| "Just email me." | "Certainly. What is the appropriate business email and may I send one summary and a possible demo time?" |
| "Not interested." | "Understood. I won't keep following up. Thank you for your time." |

## Evidence required after every founder call

- Stable hotel ID, date/time, contact role (never a guessed personal name), channel and outcome.
- Whether contact details were directly confirmed; identity and room-count discrepancies.
- Actual pain illustrated by past events, not prospecting hypotheses.
- Exact consent signal, timestamp and channel if they explicitly opted in.
- A scheduled next step, responsible owner and opt-out state.
- Hotel-specific relevant product module; don't sell feature modules the team cannot deliver.

Record only **non-personal aggregate counts and opaque CRM IDs** in GitHub.
