## Call protocol

1. Ask the person who answers whether it's okay to ask who manages guest enquiries or reservation operations.
2. Give the 30–45 second discovery pitch from [OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md](OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md); do not assert they have missed bookings.
3. Ask for one **recent actual** guest-service or booking-hand-off example, not general speculation.
4. Seek permission for a 15–20 minute discovery meeting.
5. Separately ask whether they wish to receive Worthkind Flow early-access updates; record a clear YES/NO and timestamp.
6. If offered a manager's **personal** number, enter it only into the permissioned CRM and do not share it publicly or in GitHub.
7. Mark state as contacted/declined/pending evidence based on reality; opt-ins count only with authorized role and explicit permission.
